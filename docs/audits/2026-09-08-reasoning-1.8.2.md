# Reasoning capabilities — 1.8.2

## Behavior

- Native allowed options take priority over family fallbacks, including explicit disabled/empty capability metadata.
- Toggle-only: Fast / Thinking. Effort-capable: Fast plus advertised effort levels, excluding redundant Thinking. Models without off/none do not offer Fast; unsupported models use Default.
- Aliases inherit the capabilities of the base model in their resolved connection. Changing a base or detecting models again rebuilds valid built-ins and preserves custom templates, ownership, and ordering.
- Unsupported custom effort values remain stored for portability but are omitted from requests. Replace/prepend/append system prompt handling remains intact.
- Native off/on is translated to none/medium for OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions. Effort levels remain unchanged.
- Existing deployments should detect their models again and save after upgrading to refresh legacy capability data.
